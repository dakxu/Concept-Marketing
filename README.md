# Starbucks-Aura-Energy-Market-Analysis
A market research and go-to-market strategy analysis for **Starbucks Aura Energy**, a proposed functional energy drink launch in India, built on **Statista Consumer Insights survey data** covering 1,631 energy drink consumers and benchmarked against 24,170 general Indian respondents.

## 📊 Overview
This project transforms raw consumer survey data and market share estimates into a decision-ready strategic analysis — covering audience profiling, competitive benchmarking, and positioning — to evaluate whether Starbucks should enter India's energy drink category.
The analysis answers key business questions such as:
- Who is the target energy drink consumer in India, demographically and psychographically?
- Which media and purchasing channels reach this audience most effectively?
- Who are the real competitors for a premium functional entrant — not just "any energy drink" brand?
- What are Starbucks' strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats in this category?
- How should Aura Energy be positioned, priced, and distributed at launch?

## 📁 Data source
This project uses the **Statista Consumer Insights Global Survey (June 2023)**, covering energy drink consumers in India (n = 1,631) versus all Indian respondents aged 18–64 (n = 24,170), supplemented with publicly available India energy drink market share data.

## 🗂️ Document Structure
| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| `Demographic Profile` | Age, gender, income, education, household type, and location patterns of Indian energy drink consumers |
| `Psychographics` | Core values, motivators, pain points, lifestyle interests, and political/social lean |
| `Purchasing & Media Behavior` | Digital and non-digital ad touchpoints, device usage, and social media engagement |
| `Competitive Analysis` | Competitor selection methodology and profiles of Red Bull, PepsiCo's Sting, and Monster/emerging functional entrants |
| `SWOT Analysis` | Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats for the Aura Energy entry |
| `Strategic Takeaways` | Positioning, channel priority, distribution priority, and content strategy recommendations |

## 📈 Analysis Highlights
The document includes 10+ data-driven breakdowns, such as:
- **Demographic Skew** — age, gender, and income indexing vs. the general population
- **Urbanicity & Distribution Fit** — megacity/Tier-1 concentration mapped to Starbucks' retail footprint
- **Psychographic Profile** — innovator/early-adopter index, health & fitness interest, wellness orientation
- **Media Touchpoint Ranking** — social media, video, online stores, and search engagement
- **Competitor Benchmarking** — brand equity leader, volume leader, and positioning-adjacent challenger
- **SWOT Matrix** — Starbucks-specific strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats
- **Perceptual Mapping Approach** — Price vs. Health/Functional Positioning axis rationale

## 🧠 Key Insights
- The target segment is **young, affluent, and urban** — 88% Gen Z + Millennials, core targeting window ages 25–40, concentrated in Tier-1 metros.
- There's a clear tension between **convenience** (56% want fast/convenient food) and **clean ingredients** (78% try to eat healthy, 51% avoid artificial additives) — a gap current category leaders haven't closed.
- India's energy drink market is roughly **93% controlled by Red Bull, PepsiCo, Monster, Coca-Cola, and AB InBev**, but a functional/wellness-positioned white space is emerging, validated by entrants like Huckleberry.
- Starbucks' premium brand equity and physical retail footprint are strong assets for Aura Energy, though the brand starts with **zero existing equity** in the energy/performance occasion.
